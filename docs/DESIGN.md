# AAJ Contact Scan Design System

## Visual Character

Premium operational clarity: warm white working surfaces, charcoal typography and AAJ red reserved for decisive actions. The system is light-only and restrained; its confidence comes from typography, spacing and real product artifacts rather than decorative effects.

## Tokens

- Primary red: `#c22028`; deep hover: `#9f161d`
- Charcoal: `#2c2c2c`; muted text: `#626269`
- Page ground: `#eef0f2`; quiet surface: `#f8f9fa`; white: `#ffffff`
- Success: `#278257`; warning gold: `#cca43b`; line: `#dcdde0`
- Display: Plus Jakarta Sans Variable; body/interface: Inter Variable
- Control radii: 9-12px; major panels: 16px
- Focus: 3px AAJ red with 3px offset

## Composition

Use obvious reading order, generous separation between sections and compact spacing within task groups. Content lines stay near 65-75 characters. Product imagery and synthetic business cards are evidence, not decoration.

## Interaction

Controls name concrete actions. Green, yellow and red status colors always accompany text. Motion is purposeful, brief and disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets are at least 44px.
